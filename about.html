About Us

Over the past six years, we have operated across multiple e-commerce platforms, including eBay, Kogan, Catch, and MyDeal. With the recent downturn in online retail and the closure or contraction of several marketplaces—including Catch and MyDeal—we’ve decided it’s time to pause and reassess our business direction.

As part of this transition, we are permanently closing this warehouse and will be taking a short break to restructure our operations. We are grateful for the support we've received throughout this journey and hope to return in a stronger position after this period of reflection and renewal.

About This Clearance

We are currently closing down due to internal restructuring. Our primary goal is to reduce inventory and clear warehouse space.

This is a first-come, first-serve clearance event. While we do our best to reflect inventory, please be aware that the online listing may not match real-time stock. Items are not guaranteed to be available when you view them or upon arrival.

All sales are final: no returns, refunds, or exchanges will be accepted under any circumstances.

To obtain the pickup location, you must submit a reservation through the booking form. Punctuality is essential—arriving late may result in loss of access to high-demand items.

When filling out the booking form, please list all desired items clearly, separated by commas. This helps us manage requests and prioritize based on submission order.

We appreciate your understanding during this transition.

Please note that all transactions are CASH only.
